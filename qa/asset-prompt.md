# Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: 3×3 production pose sheet for an animated Codex desktop pet

Primary request: Create one original chibi character design of Hildegard von Bingen, shown in exactly nine separate full-body poses in a precise 3×3 layout. Each cell contains the same character with identical face, costume, proportions, palette, and rendering style. The nine poses, left to right and top to bottom: 1 calm front-facing idle holding a closed illuminated manuscript; 2 brisk walking/running toward the right holding the manuscript securely; 3 peacefully asleep curled beside the manuscript; 4 friendly wave; 5 joyful small jump while holding a sprig of healing herbs; 6 startled/worried failure reaction; 7 patient waiting pose with hands folded; 8 seated writing musical notation with a quill in an open manuscript; 9 thoughtful review pose reading an open manuscript.

Subject: Hildegard von Bingen as a warm, intelligent two-head-tall chibi Benedictine abbess, mature but gentle face, black veil and white wimple, deep charcoal-brown habit with restrained moss-green and antique-gold trim, small illuminated manuscript, optional herb sprig or quill only in the specified cells. A subtle circular golden vision motif may appear behind her head but must remain attached to the character silhouette.

Style/medium: polished 2D game sprite art, Romanesque illuminated-manuscript color sensibility, clean dark outlines, soft restrained shading, charming and readable at 192×208 pixels, fully original and not copied from a specific artwork.

Composition/framing: exact 3×3 grid alignment, one isolated full-body character per equal cell, consistent scale and shared foot baseline within each row, generous padding, no overlap between cells, no dividing lines, no labels, no text, no numbers.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #FF00FF chroma-key background covering the entire canvas and every gap.

Color palette: charcoal black, warm parchment, muted moss green, antique gold, small lapis-blue accents; do not use magenta in the character.

Constraints: the background must be one perfectly uniform #FF00FF color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, reflections, or lighting variation; crisp separated edges; no cast shadows; same character identity and costume in all nine cells; all props contained within their cell.

Avoid: photorealism, modern clothing, crucifix emphasis, elaborate architecture, halos detached from the silhouette, watermark, signature, lettering, grid borders, cropped limbs, extra characters, pink or magenta subject details.
