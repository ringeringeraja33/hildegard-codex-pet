# Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: 3×3 production pose sheet for an animated Codex desktop pet

Primary request: Create one original chibi character design of Hildegard von Bingen, shown in exactly nine separate full-body poses in a precise 3×3 layout. Each cell contains the same character with identical face, costume, proportions, palette, and rendering style. The nine poses, left to right and top to bottom: 1 calm front-facing idle holding a closed illuminated manuscript and a simple upright pastoral staff; 2 brisk walking/running toward the right holding the manuscript securely; 3 peacefully asleep curled beside the manuscript; 4 natural standing wave; 5 gentle standing greeting with feet planted; 6 startled/worried failure reaction; 7 patient waiting pose with hands folded; 8 seated writing musical notation with a quill in an open manuscript; 9 thoughtful review pose reading an open manuscript. No jumping or airborne pose.

Subject: Hildegard von Bingen as a warm, intelligent two-head-tall chibi Benedictine abbess with deep forest-green eyes, a black veil and plain white wimple, and a simple charcoal-black habit with only thin antique-gold edging. A compact cluster of gold-orange-red flames sits directly above her veil in every pose to represent her visions. It is not a circular halo. Props are limited to the illuminated manuscript, pastoral staff and quill required by the listed poses.

Style/medium: polished 2D game sprite art, Romanesque illuminated-manuscript color sensibility, clean dark outlines, soft restrained shading, charming and readable at 192×208 pixels, fully original and not copied from a specific artwork.

Composition/framing: exact 3×3 grid alignment, one isolated full-body character per equal cell, consistent scale and shared foot baseline within each row, generous padding, no overlap between cells, no dividing lines, no labels, no text, no numbers.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #FF00FF chroma-key background covering the entire canvas and every gap.

Color palette: charcoal black, warm parchment, muted dark blue, restrained antique gold, deep forest-green eyes and gold-orange-red vision flames; do not use magenta in the character.

Constraints: the background must be one perfectly uniform #FF00FF color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, reflections, or lighting variation; crisp separated edges; no cast shadows; same character identity and costume in all nine cells; all props contained within their cell; every visible hand has exactly five naturally separated fingers.

Avoid: jumping, airborne poses, herbs, flowers, ornate embroidery, decorative apron panels, tassels, jeweled bands, circular or detached halos, excessive gold, brown eyes, photorealism, modern clothing, crucifix emphasis, architecture, watermark, signature, lettering, grid borders, cropped limbs, extra characters, pink or magenta subject details.

## Animation prompt

Asset type: exact 4×2 production animation sheet. Top row: four grounded standing-wave frames, with movement limited to the raised forearm, wrist and five-finger hand. Bottom row: four standing idle frames holding book and staff, with movement limited to eyelids and flame tips. Keep torso, head center, feet, scale and baseline fixed between frames. No whole-body translation, rotation, scaling, squash, bounce or sway. Use the same character, costume, colors and flat #FF00FF background as the pose sheet.
